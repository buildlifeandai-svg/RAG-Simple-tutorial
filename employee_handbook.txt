Vacation Policy
Employees receive 20 days of paid vacation each year.

Parental Leave
Employees receive 16 weeks of parental leave.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

Learning Benefit
Employees receive up to $2,000 per year for approved professional learning, certifications, conferences, and technical courses.

Health Benefits
Employees receive medical, dental, and vision insurance benefits.
